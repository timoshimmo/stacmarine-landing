ACHEIVING OUR AMBITIONS

SDG 5

Empowering Women in Maritime Leadership
We're actively breaking down barriers by promoting women in leadership and technical roles on FPSOs and within our company. This fosters a more diverse and inclusive work environment, enriching the maritime industry.

SDG 7

Pioneering Affordable & Clean Energy
We're at the forefront of decarbonization efforts.  By ensuring a sustainable flow of resources while minimizing our environmental footprint, we're paving the way for a cleaner and more affordable energy future.  We're constantly exploring innovative solutions like cleaner fuels and emission capture technologies to further this mission.

SDG 8

Creating Sustainable Jobs & Growth
Our operations generate jobs within the maritime sector, contributing to economic growth. We go beyond just creating jobs – we prioritize fair wages, safe working conditions, and continuous training opportunities for our employees, fostering a thriving and empowered workforce.

SDG 13

Combatting Climate Change Through Action
We're making a tangible difference by implementing operational efficiency improvements that reduce carbon emissions.  Additionally, we're actively exploring the integration of renewable energy sources into FPSO operations.

SDG 14

Protecting Our Oceans
We take environmental responsibility seriously.  By implementing stricter environmental protocols, we're minimizing pollution and waste discharge from FPSOs. Furthermore, we're actively supporting research and development initiatives that focus on minimizing the environmental impact of offshore operations on marine life.
We are not just delivering energy.

We're leading a purposeful transformation towards a sustainable future for our planet and its people.

About STAC Maritime

STAC is a wholly Nigerian owned company specializing in the acquisition, operation and maintenance of Floating, Production, Storage and Offloading (FPSO) vessels, Floating Storage and Offloading (FSO) vessels, Marine and offshore platforms. STAC Marine will leverage on its experienced management team and technical partners to provide a full range operations and maintenance services for the IOCs.

We have engaged and signed a Technical Service Agreement with V-ship Offshore (a global player in the provision of O&M services) to further strengthen our technical capability and development of local capacity.

STAC acquired FPSO Mayo Abo, from BW Offshore, through syndicate financing. STAC has entered into a 4 years contract, with option to extend, with Nigerian Agip Exploration (NAE) to provide services to lease and operate FPSO Mayo Abo at OML 130. STAC Marine Services offers
01.

FPSO& FSO Ownership and Operation

STAC Marine owns and operates the FPSO Mayo Abo, providing our client with flexible and scalable solutions for offshore production and storage needs.
02.

Offshore Operations Support

STAC Marine offers comprehensive support services for offshore operations, including maintenance, and technical assistance. Our experienced team ensures the smooth and efficient operation of FPSO & FSO units, optimizing uptime and minimizing downtime.
03.

Project Management

STAC Marine provides end-to-end project management services, from concept development and technical support to execution and commissioning. We work closely with clients to customize solutions that meet their specific project objectives, budget constraints, and regulatory requirements.
04.

Engineering and Design

Our in-house engineering and design team specializes in the development of FPSO & FSO solutions tailored to the unique challenges of offshore environments. We leverage cutting-edge technologies and industry best practices to optimize design efficiency, safety, and performance.
05.

Decommissioning

We can co-ordinate and supervise the complete process of decommissioning, unmooring, tow, clean up and recycling in compliance with the Hong Kong Convention of offshore units.
